Good to know

How does it work?

Enter a keyword, name or quote and choose your collections. ReactionHub opens a video search on X using those accounts. Leave the search blank to browse.

Do I need an account?

No ReactionHub account is needed. X (formerly Twitter) may ask you to sign in to see the results.

Can I add my own accounts?

Yes. Use “Edit my accounts” to save X usernames in this browser, then select “My accounts” to include them in your search.

Why am I getting no results?

Try a shorter phrase or fewer collections, and check that you’re signed into X. Results depend on X’s search.

Is ReactionHub free?

Yes. It’s a small, free tool made by Emanuele. Search results live on X.
