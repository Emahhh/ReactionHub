Frequently asked questions.

How do I find reaction videos on X (Twitter)?

Type a keyword, name or quote, choose a collection, and press Search. ReactionHub creates a targeted X search using selected reaction video accounts. The results open on X, where you can view and share the original posts.

Does ReactionHub still work with Twitter?

Twitter is now called X. ReactionHub searches public posts on X using its search operators, including videos from stan Twitter, Italian and K-pop accounts. X search availability can change, and you may need to sign in to view results.

Can I search my favorite Twitter accounts?

Yes. Select “Edit my accounts” to save your own list of X usernames. Your accounts are saved only in this browser, and you can combine them with the curated collections.

Why am I getting no results on X?

X sometimes returns few or no results for long searches. Try a shorter keyword, fewer collections, or just one account group. Make sure you are signed into X. ReactionHub doesn't control which posts X includes in its search results.

Is ReactionHub free?

Yes. ReactionHub is a free, open-source tool made by Emanuele. It doesn't host or download videos, and you don't need a ReactionHub account. Searches are carried out on X.
